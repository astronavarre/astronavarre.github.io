I am a data scientist with a PhD in Astrophysics. I spent five years in my PhD extracting meaningful signals from noisy, high-dimensional data -- the same problem that is at the heart of R&D, production, logistics, and more. My background is in observational astronomy: a field which required both highly-advanced physics knowledge and technical rigor. I spent as much time thinking about physics in far off galaxies as I did writing Python code and troubleshooting programs on Linux.
